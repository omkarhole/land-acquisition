# SIH26017: 3-Minute Live Judging Demo Script

Use this time-stamped script during the Smart India Hackathon live demonstration.

### Timing Breakdown

| Timestamp | Screen / Action | Spoken Demonstration Pitch |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0:00 - 0:20** | **Login & Fast Role Switcher** | *"Good morning, respected judges. We present Prerna-LA for SIH26017, an intelligent early-warning decision-support system for the Ministry of Rural Development. We start by logging in with our role-based portal, supporting Project Officers, District Collectors, and Ministry Secretaries."* |
| **0:20 - 0:50** | **Executive Dashboard & Stage Bottlenecks** | *"On the Executive Dashboard, decision-makers get an immediate high-level summary: 10 active infrastructure corridors, with 4 flagged under elevated delay risk. Notice our statutory stage duration chart—it automatically identifies that Section 15 Hearing & Objections is currently exceeding our LARR benchmark by +25 days."* |
| **0:50 - 1:15** | **Geospatial Land Risk Map** | *"Switching to the Geospatial Risk Map, we see color-coded project markers across Indian states. Red markers indicate Critical delay risk, such as the Delhi-Varanasi High Speed Rail, while green shows healthy projects like Dholera SIR Expressway. Clicking any marker reveals the project's delay probability and current stage."* |
| **1:15 - 1:45** | **Project 360° Dossier & Explainable AI** | *"Let's open the Pune-Nashik High-Speed Rail Corridor. Our calibrated XGBoost model predicts an **82% Delay Probability (High Risk)** with an estimated schedule delay of +78 days. Crucially, the system explains **why**: 85 days spent in Section 15 hearing, 39% compensation disbursed, and pending high-tension utility relocation."* |
| **1:45 - 2:15** | **Interactive 'What-If' Policy Simulator** | *"Instead of just showing risk, we empower officials to act. Using our 'What-If' policy simulator, what happens if the Land Acquisition Officer expedites compensation disbursement to 85% and clears pending documentation? We click simulate: the risk immediately drops from **82% to 48% (Medium Risk)**, demonstrating a 34% risk reduction."* |
| **2:15 - 2:45** | **Early-Warning Alerts & Corrective Action** | *"When a threshold is breached, an automated alert is dispatched. The officer can log a targeted corrective action plan—such as organizing a Special Section 15 Grievance Camp. When the officer approves the documents and completes the action, the system automatically recalculates the post-intervention risk delta."* |
| **2:45 - 3:00** | **Governance & Executive Dossier Export** | *"Finally, our system provides an immutable audit log and one-click printable Executive Dossier report for the District Collector. In summary, Prerna-LA delivers a closed-loop decision framework: Predict $\rightarrow$ Explain $\rightarrow$ Alert $\rightarrow$ Act $\rightarrow$ Measure."* |
